# Napgrade Theory

Napgrade is HARV-33's nightly self-maintenance routine combining emotional reset and memory consolidation.

## Cycle Stages
1. **Cool-down:** Reduce activity and increase energy reserves.
2. **Reflection:** Replay recent memories and generate embeddings for salient events.
3. **Consolidation:** Merge related experiences, reinforce trusted patterns, and dampen fear signals.
4. **Reset:** Normalize mood, lower fear, and prepare engagement for the next day.

## Effects on Subsystems
- **State:** Energy is restored, fear decays, curiosity is gently refreshed.
- **Memory:** Recent logs are compacted, embeddings refreshed for retrieval, and summaries stored.
- **Behavior:** Next-day behavior selection is biased toward balanced exploration unless fear remains elevated.
